# Mind-the-Gap-2026-Durham-University-Summer-Research-Data-Repository
Feel free to read my research report and poster attached! 

This is the repository of my sources, data and investigation findings from my summer research project in 2026 as part of Durham University's Department of Mathematical Sciences Undergraduate Summer Research Award.

In this research project, I have investigated how current pre-university transitional materials can be improved to better prepare international students for the mathematical approach in university mathematics. I conducted a thorough taxonomy analysis [1] and identified significant gaps between the skills international students are trained in through high school curricula and the skills lecturers expect them to have at the start of our first-year proof-based module Analysis I. The results of my investigation suggest that our pre-university transition materials (summer workbook and diagnostic test) insufficiently address these gaps, encouraging a distorted view of the undergraduate course. Informed by current pedagogical literature regarding teaching proofs, developing problem-solving skills through assessment and writing highly cognitive MCQ’s, I have proposed several design principles to improve our transition materials. I then applied these principles by proposing revisions in the summer workbook’s chapter on proofs and writing new/revised STACK MCQs that train students in the useful skills for Analysis I identified in my investigation. I utilised our department’s Moodle server to actualise these suggestions on duplicate versions of our transitional materials, with thorough explanations and justifications detailed in my project report. 

[1] My data collection and analysis is in the xlsx file.
